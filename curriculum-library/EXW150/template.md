# EXW150: Nutrition for Sports and Exercise

## Master Course Template | The Curriculum Architect

### 🎯 Course Intent

This course bridges the gap between physiological energy demands
and nutritional intake. It emphasizes evidence-based fueling
strategies for athletes utilizing a mobile-first, OER model.

---

### 📏 Single Source of Truth (SSoT)

* **PAGA 2018 (2nd Ed.):** Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans.
* **U.S. Dietary Guidelines for Americans:** The federal standard.

---

### 🔄 The Mission Loop Framework

#### 1. Pattern (Identify)

Students track and identify personal or client dietary habits.

#### 2. Rule (Apply)

Students apply federal macronutrient distribution ranges and
timing "rules" based on the SSoT.

#### 3. Solve (Intervene)

Students solve for a performance-based meal plan that replaces
the identified pattern with an evidence-based guideline.
