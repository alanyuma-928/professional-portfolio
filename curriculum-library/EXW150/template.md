# EXW150: Nutrition for Sports and Exercise
## Master Course Template | The Curriculum Architect

### 🎯 Course Intent
This course bridges the gap between physiological energy demands and nutritional intake. It emphasizes evidence-based fueling strategies for athletes and active individuals, utilizing a mobile-first, OER-accessible delivery model.

---

### 📏 Single Source of Truth (SSoT)
To ensure academic and clinical integrity, all nutritional prescriptions are audited against:
* **PAGA 2018 (2nd Ed.):** Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans.
* **U.S. Dietary Guidelines for Americans:** The federal standard for healthy eating patterns.

---

### 🔄 The Mission Loop Framework: Nutritional Auditing
Students move beyond "calorie counting" to analyze systemic health patterns.

#### 1. Pattern (Identify)
Students track and identify personal or client dietary habits and energy expenditure.
> **Example:** Recognizing a pattern of high-glycemic intake immediately followed by energy "crashes" during afternoon training sessions.

#### 2. Rule (Apply)
Students apply federal macronutrient distribution ranges and timing "rules" based on the SSoT.
> **Example:** Applying the rule of 1.2–2.0g of protein per kg of body weight for recovery as per ACSM/PAGA alignment.

#### 3. Solve (Intervene)
Students solve for a sustainable, performance-based meal plan that replaces the identified pattern with an evidence-based guideline.
> **Example:** Designing a pre-workout fueling strategy that optimizes glycogen stores while maintaining GI comfort.
