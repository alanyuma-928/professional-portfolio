# EXW101: Concepts of Kinesiology & Physical Education

## Master Course Template | The Curriculum Architect

### 🎯 Course Intent

This course serves as the "Front Door" to Human Movement Science.
It establishes the historical and sociological foundations of Kinesiology,
prioritizing OER accessibility for first and second-year students.

---

### 📏 Single Source of Truth (SSoT)

* **PAGA 2018 (2nd Ed.):** Federal Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans.

---

### 🔄 The Mission Loop Framework

Every module in EXW101 follows this problem-framing sequence:

#### 1. Pattern (Identify)

Students identify patterns of physical inactivity or sociological barriers
to movement within diverse populations.

#### 2. Rule (Apply)

Students apply the federal **PAGA 2018** rules to determine the
minimum dosage of activity required for health benefits.

#### 3. Solve (Intervene)

Students design a community-level intervention or personal action plan
that satisfies the Rule and breaks the Pattern.
