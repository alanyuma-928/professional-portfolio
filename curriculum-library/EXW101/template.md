# EXW101: Concepts of Kinesiology & Physical Education
## Master Course Template | The Curriculum Architect

### 🎯 Course Intent
This course serves as the "Front Door" to Human Movement Science. It establishes the historical and sociological foundations of Kinesiology, prioritizing OER accessibility for first and second-year community college students.

---

### 📏 Single Source of Truth (SSoT)
All content and clinical auditing are governed by:
* **PAGA 2018 (2nd Ed.):** Federal Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans.

---

### 🔄 The Mission Loop Framework
Every module in EXW101 follows this problem-framing sequence:

#### 1. Pattern (Identify)
Students identify patterns of physical inactivity or sociological barriers to movement within diverse populations.
> **Example:** Identifying "Exercise Deserts" in rural community health data.

#### 2. Rule (Apply)
Students apply the federal **PAGA 2018** rules to determine the minimum dosage of activity required for health benefits.
> **Example:** Applying the "150–300 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic activity" rule.

#### 3. Solve (Intervene)
Students design a community-level intervention or personal action plan that satisfies the Rule and breaks the Pattern.
> **Example:** Solving for a sustainable 10-week walking program for sedentary seniors.
