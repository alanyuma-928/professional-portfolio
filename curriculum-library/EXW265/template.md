# EXW265: Exercise for Special Populations
## Master Course Template | The Curriculum Architect

### 🎯 Course Intent
This course focuses on the design and modification of physical activity for individuals with chronic conditions, disabilities, or unique physiological needs. It prioritizes evidence-based safety and the promotion of functional independence.

---

### 📏 Single Source of Truth (SSoT)
**Strict Adherence:** Unlike other EXW courses, EXW265 utilizes the **PAGA 2018 (2nd Ed.)** as the exclusive "Single Source of Truth."
* *Note: ACSM or other clinical standards are only integrated if they maintain direct alignment with PAGA safety guidelines.*

---

### 🔄 The Mission Loop Framework: Clinical Safety Audit
Students apply the Mission Loop to move from a medical diagnosis to a safe movement prescription.

#### 1. Pattern (Identify)
Students identify the specific physiological limitations or contraindications associated with a chronic condition (e.g., Type 2 Diabetes, Hypertension, Pregnancy).
> **Example:** Identifying "Peripheral Neuropathy" as a pattern that increases fall risk in a geriatric client.

#### 2. Rule (Apply)
Students apply the specific **PAGA 2018** rules for that population to determine safety thresholds and intensity.
> **Example:** Applying the rule that individuals with chronic conditions should avoid "inactivity" and aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity activity as tolerated.

#### 3. Solve (Intervene)
Students solve for a modified exercise program that mitigates risk while achieving the PAGA-recommended "dose" of activity.
> **Example:** Solving for a seated resistance and aerobic circuit that improves strength without stressing the patient's balance.

---

### 🤖 AI Integration: "The Clinical Auditor"
The **AI Tool Kit** in EXW265 is used to audit prescriptions for safety. Students are trained to use AI to "stress test" their exercise plans against the PAGA standards.
