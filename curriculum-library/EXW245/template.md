# EXW245: Guidelines for Exercise Testing and Prescription
## Master Course Template | The Curriculum Architect

### 🎯 Course Intent
This hybrid course (Online/Onsite) provides students with the clinical skills necessary to assess human performance and prescribe evidence-based exercise. It emphasizes the "Auditor" mindset, using AI to verify physiological math before performing physical testing.

---

### 📏 Single Source of Truth (SSoT)
All metabolic calculations and risk stratifications are governed by:
* **ACSM 12th Ed.:** Guidelines for Exercise Testing and Prescription.
* **PAGA 2018 (2nd Ed.):** Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans.

---

### 🔄 The Hybrid Lab Scaffold (Part A & Part B)
This course utilizes a two-part methodology to ensure technical accuracy and clinical safety.

#### Part A: Gemini AI Guided Learning
Students utilize the **AI Tool Kit** as a simulation and auditing partner.
* **Simulation:** Modeling patient outcomes based on varying intensity levels.
* **Math Verification:** Auditing metabolic equations (VO2, METs, Heart Rate Reserve).
* **Clinical Auditing:** Pre-screening "virtual clients" for contraindications.

#### Part B: Lode Corival Practical Application
Students translate AI-verified data into real-world testing in the **AWC Yuma Campus Lab (GY 116)**.
* **Accuracy:** Utilizing the **Lode Corival** for research-grade accuracy.
* **Hyperbolic Braking:** Applying constant work-rate protocols independent of pedal speed (RPM).
* **Execution:** Performing submaximal and maximal testing protocols.



---

### 🔄 The Mission Loop Framework
#### 1. Pattern (Identify)
Identify clinical risk factors or physiological plateaus in a client profile.
#### 2. Rule (Apply)
Apply the **ACSM 12th Ed.** "Rules" for test termination and prescription intensity (FITT-VP).
#### 3. Solve (Intervene)
Solve for a customized prescription that maximizes safety and physiological adaptation.
